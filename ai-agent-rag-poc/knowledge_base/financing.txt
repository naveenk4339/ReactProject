AutoMart Financing

AutoMart offers car loans through partner banks.
The standard interest rate is 6.5 percent per year for customers with a credit score above 700.
Customers with a credit score between 600 and 700 get a rate of 9 percent per year.
Loan terms of 36, 48, 60 or 72 months are available.
A minimum down payment of 10 percent of the car price is required.
