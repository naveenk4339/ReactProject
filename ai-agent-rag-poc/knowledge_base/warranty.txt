AutoMart Warranty

Every new car comes with a 3 year or 36,000 mile bumper-to-bumper warranty, whichever comes first.
Used cars come with a 90 day powertrain warranty covering the engine and transmission.
The warranty does not cover tires, brake pads, wiper blades or damage from accidents.
An extended warranty of 5 years can be purchased for 1,200 dollars at checkout.
